## Finding

**PDF thumbs do not appear when the pointer rests over the idle thumb.** Open the budget appendix PDF, choose Original and Scrolling, and use the zoom controls to reach 190% in a 1440 × 960 reader window. Wheel down, observe the thumbs at the right and bottom edges, and pause until they fade. Bring the pointer to the middle of either previous thumb, click and release to place it precisely, and leave the pointer there. Expect the thumb to reappear at the same offset. In both axes the edge pixels and the scroll offset remained identical to idle. Native vertical and horizontal drags from those same positions moved the respective offsets, confirming the hit targets. In the first run, horizontal scroll-wheel input through the adapter left the horizontal offset unchanged, so horizontal motion was exercised directly by dragging the native thumb.

The first failure is preserved in `30-pdf-continuous-original-active.png`, `30-pdf-continuous-original-idle.png`, `30-pdf-continuous-original-hover-v.png`, and `30-pdf-continuous-original-hover-h.png`. The horizontal drag moved scrollLeft from 502 to 657; the vertical drag moved scrollTop from 371.5 to 10803.5. The same procedure reproduced the same hover behavior in the other themes and in Paginated at 190%. For example, the fixed-layout Dark evidence includes `43-pdf-fixed-dark-right-edge-active-3x.png`, `43-pdf-fixed-dark-right-edge-idle-3x.png`, the matching bottom-edge crops, and `43-pdf-fixed-dark-hover-v-settled.png` and `43-pdf-fixed-dark-hover-h-settled.png`. `43-pdf-fixed-dark-pixels.txt` records the edge comparisons. The same post-release positioning method revealed the EPUB thumb as a positive control.
